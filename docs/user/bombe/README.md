# The Bombe

[Back to the contents](../README.md)

The Bombe was the electromechanical machine that Bletchley Park used to find the daily Enigma key. It tests a crib, a guessed piece of plaintext, against the cipher text at every rotor position and stops at the positions where the guess could be right.

1. [How the Bombe broke Enigma](history.md): the Bombe's history, cribs, menus and loops, the diagonal board, the middle rotor under the crib, and Banburismus.
2. [Using the Bombe](using-the-bombe.md): the Hut 8 notebook, the Bombe, the checking machine, and reading the message, its indicators and the day's traffic.
3. [Breaking a message](breaking-a-message.md): a worked example on Carter's weather crib, and an outline of an exercise.
4. [Breaking the M4](breaking-the-m4.md): how Shark was broken with weather short signals, re-encodements and four-rotor Bombes.

The words in the messages are explained in the [glossary](../glossary.md#terms-in-the-messages).

## Switching modes

The title at the top left switches between the two modes. The active mode is in capitals; click the other one to switch. Each mode keeps its state while the other is shown. While an Enigma key is held down, the mode cannot be switched.

In Bombe mode, the book button opens these instructions.
