# The machine

[Back to The Enigma](README.md)

## Header

- The title switches between Enigma and [Bombe](../bombe/README.md) mode.
- **Model** switches between the M3 and the M4. The machine resets, so the application asks first.
- The summary shows the model, the reflector (UKW) and the rotors from left to right.
- **Key** shows the applied daily key and its Grundstellung. It says "(modified)" once the reflector, rotors, rings or plugs differ from the key. Turning the rotors does not count.
- The buttons at the right open these instructions and the [operator's notebook](operator-mode.md), each in its own window, and the [Key sheets](key-sheets.md) panel.

Use the tabs on the left, or Arrow Up/Down on a focused tab, to switch views.

## Top-down

The machine seen from above: rotor windows, lampboard and keyboard.

- Press a key with the mouse or the physical keyboard. The rotors step first, then a lamp lights while the key is held down.
- Only one key can be held at a time. While it is held, the machine cannot be changed.
- Turn a rotor with its thumbwheel (click the upper or lower half), the mouse wheel over the window, or Arrow Up/Down on a focused window.

The physical keyboard types only in this view.

## Open lid

The machine seen from the front, with the rotor box and the reflector box below it.

- **Insert or swap a rotor:** click a rotor in the box, then a slot. A rotor already in the slot goes back to the box.
- **Remove a rotor:** click it in the machine.
- **Turn a rotor:** use the mouse wheel or Arrow Up/Down on a rotor in the machine.
- **Set a ring (Ringstellung):** use the mouse wheel or Arrow Up/Down on a rotor in the box. Rings can only be set outside the machine.
- **Change the reflector:** click the spare reflector.

The M3 takes three of rotors I–VIII and reflector B or C. The M4 adds Beta or Gamma in the Greek slot, at the left, and takes B-thin or C-thin. The Greek rotor never steps.

## Plugboard

The sockets in keyboard order. Up to 13 cables, each letter in at most one pair.

- **Connect:** drag a cable between two sockets, or pick two sockets in turn with the mouse or Enter/Space.
- **Disconnect:** click a plugged socket.
- **Move:** drag a plug to another socket, or off the board to remove the cable.

The list below the board shows all cables.
