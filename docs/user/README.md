# Enigma user guide

This application simulates the Enigma M3 and M4 used by the Kriegsmarine, and the Bombe that broke it. It covers the machine, the monthly key sheets, the Navy indicator procedure for sending and receiving messages, and breaking a message with the Bombe.

These instructions open in their own window with the book button at the top right of the machine window. The contents beside the page open any page; click ▸ to open a section and ▾ to close it, or hide the contents with **Hide contents** and bring them back with the bar on the left. Examples of what the operator writes down are handwritten, as in the operator's notebook.

## Contents

1. [The Enigma](enigma/README.md): the machine, key sheets, sending and receiving messages by hand or with the operator's notebook, the officer keys and weather short signals.
2. [The Bombe](bombe/README.md): how the Bombe broke Enigma, breaking a message in Bombe mode, and how the M4 was broken.
3. [Glossary](glossary.md): the German terms used in the application, and the words and abbreviations in the Bombe's messages.

## Quick start

1. Open **Key sheets** (the panel button at the top right) and click **Generate**. Choose a model and a year, and click **Generate**. Then choose a sheet, such as Triton for this month.
2. Click **Apply** on a day. The machine is set up with that day's key.
3. Open the **operator's notebook** (the message pad button) and click **Send a message**. Follow the steps.
4. Copy the radio message, click **New message**, open the **Decrypt** tab, paste it under **Received message** and receive it again.

When you know the steps, try them without the notebook: see [Sending by hand](enigma/sending-by-hand.md).
