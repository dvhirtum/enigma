# Enigma

A simulator of the Kriegsmarine Enigma M3 and M4, with historically exact wirings, and of the Turing-Welchman Bombe used at Bletchley Park to break it.

[Download the Windows installer](https://github.com/dvhirtum/enigma/releases/latest) · [User guide](guide/) · [Source](https://github.com/dvhirtum/enigma)

## Enigma mode

Set the machine up from key sheets and send and receive messages, following the German Navy procedures: the indicator system, officer keys and weather short signals.

## Bombe mode

Break a message the way Hut 8 did. The Hut 8 notebook takes you from a crib to a menu, then to the stops and the key. The machine window shows the Bombe's front, side and back, with drums to fit and cables to plug, and the checking machine. Messages come from historical intercepts, generated exercises or pasted text.
